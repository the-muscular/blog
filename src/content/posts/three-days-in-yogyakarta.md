Example post. I went with no plan except to walk everywhere I could.

## What I'd do again

- **Get up for the sunrise,** even when the alarm feels like a crime.
- **Eat breakfast where the locals queue,** not where the reviews say.
- **Leave one afternoon empty.** It became my favourite part of the trip.

> The best plan was having almost no plan.

I saved every place I liked on [a shared map](https://maps.google.com), in case you're going soon.
